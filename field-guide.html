What We Do — And What We Don’t

We run the competition, not the whole event. Knowing the line up front is what keeps a tournament day from having gaps nobody owns.

We handle

  • ✓Setting up your registration form, if you want one built.
  • ✓The bracket draw — run in the open, and re-checkable afterwards by anyone.
  • ✓The playing schedule, built around your courts, your hours and your format.
  • ✓Match scoresheets and schedule printouts for the desk and the noticeboard.
  • ✓Match control on the day: calling matches to courts, keeping scores current, handling reshuffles, and helping arbitrate when something is disputed.
  • ✓Live scores and standings as play happens, and the final podium results at the end.

We don’t

  • ✗Run registration or collect entry fees. The form we can build; the sign-ups and the money stay with you.
  • ✗Book the venue, the courts or the equipment.
  • ✗Officiate. We record results; we don’t referee or call lines.
  • ✗Make the final call on a dispute. We advise and help arbitrate — the tournament director decides.
  • ✗Supply prizes, medals, trophies or giveaways.
  • ✗Handle food, marketing, sponsorship or the event’s own promotion.
