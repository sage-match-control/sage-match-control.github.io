Pre-Tournament Prep

Used before the event runs

Scoresheet Generator

Upload matchups, print scoresheets in four styles (Standard, No Referee, No Referee Wide, Best of 3), plus blank sheets on standby.

Live

Tournament Time Calculator

Plug in courts, match length, and buffer time; see match count and finish time instantly. Dual meets also hand the finished plan straight to the sheet generator below.

Live

Bracket Generator

Paste in a category's pairs, set how many brackets, and let the draw run on screen. Exports a printable image or a plain-text list, stamped with the event name.

Live

Dual Meet Sheet Generator

Builds an event workbook from a calculator plan — every category tab, plus Variables, Title and Reference for Players — instead of copying last event's sheet and find-replacing team codes. Runs inside your own copy of the SAGE Dual Meet Master workbook. Player names and the match schedule are still added by hand.
